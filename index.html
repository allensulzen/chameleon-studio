Flash pedal over WebUSB

  1. Connect the pedal with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Enter DFU mode: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.
  3. Click connect and pick STM32 BOOTLOADER / DFU in FS Mode.

Streams firmware/chameleon.bin (built from firmware/ with make) into the Seed's internal flash over DfuSe, then reboots it. The current image is the first-flash build: control surface + Dynamic Overdrive on patches A/B, C/D pass-through.

Generated firmware
