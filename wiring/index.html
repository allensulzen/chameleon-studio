Chameleon pedal · breadboard bring-up · Daisy Seed

Breadboard sheet

One evening's build: seat the Seed, wire the footswitch, three pots and two jacks, and hear the first overdrive. Everything on this sheet matches the firmware already on the Seed, so each step has something you can see or hear when it's right.

Pin numbers on this sheet are the physical header pins (1–40), counted the way the board is printed. The older project plan mislabelled three of them: pin 21 is 3V3 analog, not ground; pin 38 is 3V3 digital; the ground you want for the footswitch and power is pin 40. The table below is the one to trust.

What you're building tonight

The Seed does all the audio work. Around it: a momentary footswitch that the firmware reads as tap (bypass) or hold (next patch), three 10 kΩ pots read as 0–3.3 V, and two ¼″ jacks straight into the Seed's line-level in and out. No op-amp buffer yet — plug the guitar straight in for tonight; it works, just a touch dull. Power comes over the USB cable from the laptop, so leave the 9 V jack and diode for another night.

Put pin 21 on the breadboard's red rail and pin 20 on the blue rail; then the pots are three wires each to the rails plus one wiper wire back to the Seed. Keep pin 40 (digital ground) for the footswitch only — the two grounds are joined inside the Seed, but keeping the audio ground on its own rail is why the pedal will be quiet later.

Build order, with a check after each step

  1. Seat the Seed and power it

    Push the Seed into the breadboard straddling the centre channel, USB socket toward one end. Each pin gets two free holes beside it. Plug the USB-C cable into the laptop.

    Check Two LEDs on solid: the small power LED near the USB socket and the user LED near pin 20. Solid user LED means the firmware booted and patch A is active. If only the power LED lights, the flash didn't take — re-flash from the studio.

  2. Footswitch

    Two wires: pin 28 to one lug of the momentary switch, pin 40 to another lug. On a soft-touch SPST that's the only two lugs. If the switch in the parts pile is a 3PDT latching stomp, don't use it tonight — a jumper wire touched from pin 28 to pin 40 does the same job for testing.

    Check Tap: user LED goes off (bypass). Tap again: back on. Hold about a second: the LED blinks 2 times — you're on patch B. Hold again: 3 blinks (C), then 4 (D), then 1 (A).

  3. Pots

    Each 10 kΩ pot has three lugs. Looking at the back of the pot with the lugs pointing down: left lug to the blue rail (AGND 20), right lug to the red rail (3V3A 21), middle lug (the wiper) to the Seed — pot 1 to pin 22, pot 2 to 23, pot 3 to 24. If a pot turns the "wrong way" later, swap its two outer lugs.

    Check Nothing to see until audio is in — but a multimeter on a wiper should sweep 0 → 3.3 V as the pot turns. With no pots wired the firmware simply keeps the programmed values, so a missing pot never breaks anything.

  4. Jacks and first sound

    Guitar jack: tip to pin 16, sleeve to the blue rail. Amp jack: tip to pin 18, sleeve to the blue rail. Chassis jacks have a tip lug (the long one that touches the plug's end) and a sleeve lug on the body. Turn the amp's volume all the way down before plugging in — the Seed puts out line level, hotter than a guitar.

    Check Play. Patch A is a mild overdrive with the pots on Gain / Tone / Level; patch B is the same overdrive hotter and darker; C and D are clean pass-through so you can A/B against the dry guitar by holding the footswitch. Tap = true bypass with the LED off.

  5. Not tonight

    The TL072 input buffer (fixes the slight dullness of plugging a passive pickup straight into a line input), the 9 V barrel jack with its 1N5817 diode and 470 µF cap, and the copper shielding all belong to the perfboard build. The wiring above is exactly what carries over; the buffer just slots between the guitar jack and pin 16.

If something's off

  • No user LED, power LED on. The bootloader is still in charge. Hold BOOT, tap RESET, release, and flash again from chameleonpedal.com → Flash Pedal.
  • Tap does nothing. The footswitch wire is on the wrong pin — it must be pin 28 (D21) on the right-hand column, 13th from the top, to pin 40 at the very top right.
  • Hum or buzz. Expected on a breadboard with a plastic-cased laptop supply; keep the jack sleeves on the analog rail and the guitar cable short. The metal enclosure and shielding fix it for good.
  • Sound but a pot does nothing. Its wiper is on the wrong pin, or the pot is on the patch's third parameter that barely changes (Level on patch A). Turn it fully and listen for volume.
  • Distorted on the clean patches (C/D). Input is clipping: humbuckers hit the Seed's line input hard. Back the guitar's volume knob off; the buffer stage handles this properly later.

Checking from the Mac

The Seed only shows up as a USB device while it's in DFU mode; once the firmware runs it disappears from the USB list, which is how you know it left the bootloader.

system_profiler SPUSBDataType | grep -A6 -i "DFU"     # in DFU mode: shows "DFU in FS Mode", vendor 0x0483
system_profiler SPUSBDataType | grep -ci "DFU"        # after flashing: 0 — the Seed is running Chameleon
